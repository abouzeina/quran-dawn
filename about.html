Quran Dawn is an online Quran academy built around a simple idea: learning becomes more meaningful when it feels personal, approachable, and consistent.

From the first Arabic letter to confident recitation, our learning paths make room for different starting points. With individual guidance and time to practise, each lesson is a step towards a lasting connection with the Quran.
