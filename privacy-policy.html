Overview

This page outlines privacy topics for learners and families contacting Quran Dawn or exploring our online Quran lessons.

Information you share

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, course interests, and a message. Please share only the information needed for your enquiry and avoid sending payment card details or sensitive documents by email.

Our contact form prepares a draft in your email app. You can review that draft before sending it to learn@qurandawn.com.

Enquiries and learning support

Information provided in an enquiry helps our team understand your question and discuss suitable courses, packages, or lesson arrangements. Ask our team about the information required before enrolling yourself or your child.

Website and external services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to that service to display the site typography. If you use the contact form, your chosen email provider handles the email you send.

External services operate under their own privacy policies. Details of any lesson or payment services should be confirmed before you use them.

Children and family enquiries

A parent or guardian should handle enquiries and enrolment arrangements for a child. When asking about lessons, provide only the details necessary to discuss the child's learning needs.

Questions about your information

To ask about personal information you have shared, request a correction, or discuss deletion, email learn@qurandawn.com. Describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive information.

Policy details and updates

The final policy will set out confirmed data handling practices, including retention, service providers, and applicable privacy rights. Please contact us if you need clarification before sharing personal information.
