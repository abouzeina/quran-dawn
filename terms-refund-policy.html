Overview

These draft terms cover the main topics to discuss when arranging online Quran lessons with Quran Dawn. Confirm your package details and the applicable terms with our team before making a payment.

Enrolment and lessons

Review the course, lesson length, frequency, time zone, and learning requirements with our team before enrolling. A parent or guardian should arrange enrolment for learners under 18.

Online lessons require a suitable device, a working internet connection, and a quiet space where the learner can participate.

Packages and payments

Explore the available options on our Packages page. Before paying, confirm the total price, currency, number of lessons, payment schedule, and any renewal or expiry conditions.

Keep your payment confirmation and the written details of the package you selected.

Scheduling and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel a lesson, contact our team as early as possible. Ask about the required notice, missed lessons, and rescheduling arrangements before booking.

If a teacher cannot attend, contact us to confirm the arrangements for that lesson.

Refund enquiries

To request a refund review, email learn@qurandawn.com with your enrolment email, package name, payment date or reference, and the reason for your request. Do not include full card details.

Refund eligibility, request deadlines, deductions, and processing times have not yet been specified in this draft. Obtain the approved refund terms from our team before purchasing a package.

A respectful learning environment

Please treat teachers, learners, and staff respectfully. Learning materials are intended to support your lessons. Ask permission before recording a session or sharing materials, recordings, or another person's information.

Questions and policy updates

For clarification about a package, a lesson, or these draft terms, contact our team or email learn@qurandawn.com. Keep a copy of the terms confirmed for your booking.
